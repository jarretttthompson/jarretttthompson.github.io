Lighting Console

Obsidian NX1

Power up and load the show

When Arrival / openingWho Anyone opening
  1. If the console is off, turn it on.
  2. Load the show file Southern Social Prime.
  3. Check the Bank display A reads Bank 1. If it does not, use the ∨ ∧ keys under it.
  4. Push up all ten faders in Bank 1.
Watch out
  • Fader 10 B is the Pre-Show Blue override. While it is up the lights are locked to blue and the color cannot be changed. Pull fader 10 down to release the colors.
  • To pick a static color, pull fader 10 B down, then press one of the color buttons C on the Playback Buttons page. Rows are fixture groups, columns are colors.

Hazers on at 9 PM

When 9:00 PM, one hour before show timeWho Sub engineer or whoever is at the booth
  1. At 9 PM, open the Playback Buttons page on the touchscreen.
  2. Press Haze 5% D. It reads 100% once it is running.

Closing: lights back up for cleanup

When End of nightWho Anyone closing
  1. Wait until the bar's work lights have been turned back on.
  2. On the far-left column of the console, press Snap E.
  3. Then press Rel E, right next to it.
Booth

Audio Console

Midas M32R

House music

When Open to close, whenever there is no act onWho Anyone at the booth
  1. Press AUX IN USB A in the Fader Layer block at the top right.
  2. House music is the fourth fader from the left, labelled iPad B.
  3. Make sure its MUTE button C is not lit, then set the level with the fader.

Game days: switching between TV and music

When Game days, at kickoff and every commercial breakWho Whoever is assigned to the booth
  1. Make sure the left faders are on the AUX IN USB layer A. iPad B and Tv D sit side by side, fourth and fifth from the left.
  2. Game on: pull the iPad fader B all the way down, then push the Tv fader D up to its level.
  3. Commercials: pull the Tv fader D all the way down, then push the iPad fader B back up to its level.
  4. Always bring the one that is playing down before bringing the other one up.
Watch out
  • Only one of the two should be up at a time, or the game and the music will play over each other.
  • Both MUTE buttons should stay unlit. If a fader is up and nothing is coming out, check its MUTE first.
Booth and TV wall

TV Wall

Blackmagic ATEM Mini Pro, two OREI HDMI-over-Ethernet extenders, VIDEO WALL INPUTS remote

Game or cable TV on the wall

When Game daysWho Anyone
  1. Press 2 F on the VIDEO WALL INPUTS remote. Apple TV is 3 H.

Band graphics from a computer in the booth (input 1)

When Before the showWho Sub engineer
  1. Plug the band's graphics computer (PC or Mac) into HDMI IN 1 A on the ATEM.
  2. Press the big 1 button C on the ATEM so it lights red. That puts input 1 on the wall.
  3. Check that FTB E is not flashing. If it is, the wall is faded to black: press FTB once to fade back in.
  4. Press 4 G on the VIDEO WALL INPUTS remote.
Watch out
  • If the wall shows nothing, check in this order: remote on 4, the right program button lit red on the ATEM, FTB not flashing, HDMI seated.

Band graphics from a computer on stage (input 2)

When Before the showWho Sub engineer
  1. Get the OREI sender from the basket behind the light board.
  2. On stage, find the Ethernet cable dangling behind the bottom-right TV and plug it into the sender.
  3. Plug the band's computer into the sender with an HDMI cable.
  4. On the ATEM, press the big 2 button D so it lights red. The stage feed arrives on HDMI IN 2 B.
  5. Check that FTB E is not flashing, and that the remote is on 4 G.
Watch out
  • Put the sender back in the basket behind the light board at the end of the night.

No graphics from the band? Use shaderBoi

When Before the showWho Sub engineer
  1. On the graphics computer, open thejrummer.art/shaderBoi in a browser and make it full screen.
  2. Get a logo file from the band.
  3. Drag and drop the logo onto the shaderBoi window.
  4. Then follow the booth computer steps above (input 1).

Changing sources: always fade to black

When Any time you swap or unplug a sourceWho Sub engineer
  1. Press FTB E. It flashes while the wall is black.
  2. Make the change: swap the HDMI, switch program inputs, or set up the next computer.
  3. Press FTB E again to fade back in.
Watch out
  • Never pull the HDMI out of a live source. The wall flashes and glitches. Fade to black first, every time.

Posters

One per location, printed on letter paper and posted next to the gear. Use the Print posters button above; only the posters print.
